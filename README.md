# Treakie

**Treakie** is a personal productivity and routine-management website designed to help users organize their **study schedules, daily activities, and local routines** in one convenient platform.

The main goal of the website is to make it easier for students and individuals to build a structured routine, keep track of their daily tasks, and stay consistent with their goals. Along with traditional tracking features, Self-Tracker integrates an **AI-powered chatbot** that can provide personalized tips, suggestions, and guidance to help users improve their productivity and study habits.

## 🌐 Live Website

**Treakie:**
https://treakie.vercel.app/

## ✨ Features

### 📚 Study Schedule Management

Treakie helps users organize their study activities and maintain a structured study schedule. Users can plan their study sessions and keep track of the tasks they need to complete.

### 🗓️ Daily Routine Tracking

The website can be used to maintain a personal daily routine by keeping track of important activities and tasks. This helps users develop consistency and better understand how they spend their time.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Chatbot

One of the key features of Treakie is its integrated **AI chatbot**. The chatbot acts as a virtual productivity assistant that users can interact with whenever they need guidance or suggestions.

It can be used to get:

* Study tips
* Productivity suggestions
* Routine-improvement ideas
* Motivation and general guidance
* Suggestions for managing study time
* Help with maintaining consistency

### 💡 AI-Based Tips

The AI component can provide useful tips based on the user's needs and routine. Instead of simply tracking activities, the website aims to help users understand their habits and make better decisions about their study and daily schedule.

### 📈 Progress & Self-Monitoring

Treakie focuses on helping users monitor their progress over time. By regularly recording their activities and following a planned routine, users can develop a better understanding of their productivity and identify areas where they can improve.

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project

Many students struggle with maintaining a consistent study routine while also managing their everyday activities. Self-Tracker was created to provide a simple platform where users can combine **planning, tracking, and AI-assisted guidance**.

Rather than using separate applications for schedules, routines, and productivity advice, Self-Tracker brings these concepts together into a single website.

## 🧠 AI Integration

The AI chatbot is designed to make the application more interactive and personalized. Users can communicate with the chatbot naturally instead of relying only on predefined options.

The chatbot can serve as a personal assistant for productivity-related queries and provide suggestions that can help users organize their study time and daily activities more effectively.

## 💻 Technology

The project is implemented as a web-based application and is deployed using **Netlify**.

The application combines a user-friendly interface with productivity-tracking functionality and AI-assisted features to create an interactive self-management experience.
